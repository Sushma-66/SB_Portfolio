Hotel Revenue & Demand Analysis

An Excel-first revenue-management workbook — occupancy/ADR/RevPAR reporting, booking pace, group displacement evaluation, comp-set positioning and forecasting for a synthetic 300-room city-centre hotel.

Techniques used
  • ~2,400 live formulas (SUMIFS, AVERAGEIF, INDEX/MATCH) across daily, weekly and monthly reporting cadences — nothing hardcoded
  • Booking-pace analysis by lead-time bucket, comparing two months at matching days-before-arrival checkpoints

"Testing five sample group booking requests, four were straightforwardly worth accepting. EOD Report Automation Pipeline

Replaces a manual, error-prone end-of-day reporting process for a multi-branch restaurant group's Lightspeed POS system with a repeatable, validated pipeline that produces formatted Word reports automatically.

Techniques used
  • pandas ingestion with cross-validation of CSV-derived totals against an independent PDF branch summary (pdfplumber)
  • Row-level reconciliation — every net amount checked against gross minus discount, mismatches flagged not silently accepted
  • Two-stage templated rendering: docxtpl for scalar fields, python-docx for the dynamic table (docxtpl's row-loop syntax proved brittle)
  • pytest smoke and validation tests; pipeline halts loudly on missing files or columns rather than reporting incomplete data
Why it matters

A genuine engineering trade-off, not a shortcut: splitting rendering into two stages costs a little complexity for a lot more reliability once the template gets edited by someone else.

PythonSciPyPandasMatplotlib

Promotional Impact Statistical Analysis

Did a 3-week restaurant promotion actually work, or was the uplift just noise? Hypothesis testing on 90 days of branch-level sales for a fictional 4-branch restaurant group.

Techniques used
  • Independent two-sample t-test with a 95% confidence interval on daily net sales, promo vs. non-promo
  • Confound control — matched comparison by day-of-week, since weekends are busier regardless of any promotion
  • Pearson correlation — discount depth vs. transaction uplift, to separate correlation from causation
  • Chi-square test on category mix shift (share of items sold, promo vs. non-promo)
Key finding

A statistically significant £102.40/day uplift (p = 0.022), strengthening to p = 0.0028 once day-of-week seasonality is controlled for. Discount depth itself did not correlate significantly with uplift — the promotion's presence mattered more than its generosity.

Sponsor-Verified Analyst Job Matcher

A data pipeline that cross-references live UK job postings against the Home Office's official Register of Licensed Sponsors — built to solve the author's own visa-constrained job search.

Techniques used
  • Multi-source ETL — live job postings via Apify's scraper actor, plus the daily-republished GOV.UK sponsor register as an open-data source
  • Fuzzy string matching (RapidFuzz) to reconcile legal entity names ("HSBC Bank Plc") against job-board names ("HSBC")
  • Regex-based skill/keyword extraction from unstructured job description text
  • Configurable, multi-condition business-rule filtering (job title, location, skill match count, posting age)
Why it matters

The same reconciliation pattern used in enterprise CRM deduplication and vendor compliance checks — matching two independently-maintained, messy datasets where entity names don't align cleanly.
